USB

Headset dongles, USB mics and keyboard/mouse receivers share full-speed lanes (12 Mbit/s): a USB 1.1 controller, or one hub's translator. Audio devices reserve part of their lane; {{ u.crowded }} on one lane don't fit, and the last one stays silent. Put audio devices on ports of different lanes, and receivers behind a hub.
